Questions owners ask.

Will it annoy my customers?

It only texts people who enquired with you, it says who it is, and it stops the moment someone says no or STOP. If someone says "not yet", it asks when would be better and comes back then.

Does it phone anyone?

No. It texts only. Your team already tried calling, and most people would rather reply to a text in their own time.

What counts as a qualified appointment?

The homeowner or decision maker, in your area, booked into a real slot and reconfirmed the day before. We agree the details with you in writing before we start, and that's what we bill against.

What if my leads came from a lead supplier?

We can't text leads you bought or got from a comparison site. We work only the leads you collected yourself, through your own website, ads, showroom or phone line.

What do you need from me?

A 30-minute call, an export of your old leads (a spreadsheet is fine) and access to the diary we book into. Most of the work is ours.

What if a customer doesn't show up?

Tell us within 72 hours and you don't pay for it. If your rep cancels or can't make it, the appointment still counts.
