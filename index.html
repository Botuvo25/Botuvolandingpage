Questions people ask

What's in the free audit?

We contact your business a few times after hours and at the weekend, the way a customer would, and send one enquiry through your website. Then we time the reply. You get a one-page report on what happened and a rough estimate of what it could be costing you.

Will customers know they're talking to an AI?

Yes, it says so at the start of every call. Most people care far more that someone got back to them quickly. Anyone who'd rather speak to a person is passed to you.

Do I need to change my software?

No. We build around the phone line, diary, CRM and forms you already use. The pilot doesn't touch your systems at all.

What kinds of business is it for?

Any business where customers ring when you can't answer: estate and letting agents, clinics and salons, trades, solicitors and accountants. We set the questions and booking rules to match how you work.

Am I tied in?

No. Monthly plans are rolling with 30 days' notice, and anything we build for you is yours to keep using.
